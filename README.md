# Project Overview

Customer retention remains one of the most critical challenges in the telecommunications industry. Acquiring new customers is often more expensive than retaining existing ones, making churn analysis essential for sustainable business growth.

This project presents an end-to-end Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard developed using Power BI to identify the key factors influencing customer attrition, measure the financial impact of churn, and provide actionable insights for improving customer retention.

Using customer demographics, subscription plans, device preferences, satisfaction scores, revenue metrics, and churn behavior, the dashboard enables stakeholders to understand why customers leave and what strategic actions can be taken to reduce churn and improve profitability.

# 🔍 Key Insights
Customer Churn is Significantly High
   -
Overall churn rate stands at 29.4%.
Nearly 1 out of every 3 customers has left the service.
Churn increased significantly between January and March, indicating growing retention challenges.

Revenue Loss is Impacting Business Performance
   -
MTN lost approximately ₦58 million due to customer churn.
Churn is reducing overall profitability and limiting revenue growth opportunities.

Customer Satisfaction is Below Optimal Levels
   --
Average customer satisfaction score is 2.95/5.
Lower satisfaction levels strongly correlate with higher churn rates.

Competitor Offers are the Leading Churn Driver
   -
Top churn reasons identified:
* Better offers from competitors
* High call tariffs
* Costly data plans
* Poor network quality
* Poor customer service

This indicates both pricing and service quality concerns.

Certain Subscription Plans Experience Higher Churn
-
Plans with elevated churn rates include:

* 65GB Monthly Plan
* 25GB Monthly Plan
* 300GB FUP Monthly Unlimited Plan

These plans may require pricing review or value-added benefits.

Regional Churn Hotspots Exist
-
States with notably high churn rates include:

* Adamawa
* Kebbi
* Benue
* Niger
* Anambra

These regions require focused retention strategies.

Broadband and Router Users Show Higher Churn Risk
-
Customers using:

* 4G Routers
* 5G Broadband Routers
* Broadband MiFi Devices

demonstrate relatively higher churn patterns, suggesting possible network or pricing dissatisfaction.

Conduct plan-specific reviews to:

# 📌 Conclusion

The MTN Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard reveals that customer attrition is significantly affecting both customer retention and revenue performance. With a churn rate of 29.4% and approximately ₦58 million in lost revenue, immediate action is required to address the underlying causes.

The analysis shows that customer dissatisfaction, competitive pricing pressure, network quality concerns, and service-related issues are the primary drivers of churn. By implementing targeted retention strategies, improving service quality, and leveraging data-driven decision-making, MTN can reduce customer attrition, improve customer satisfaction, and strengthen long-term profitability.

This project demonstrates the power of business intelligence and data storytelling in transforming raw data into actionable insights that support strategic decision-making and sustainable business growth.
